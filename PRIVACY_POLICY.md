# Privacy Policy for DocKeep

Last updated: November 3, 2025

## Introduction

DocKeep is an open-source Android application designed for storing and organizing personal documents locally on your device. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

## Data Collection

DocKeep does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data or usage statistics. All documents and images managed by the app are stored locally on your device and never transmitted over the internet.

## Permissions Used

The app requests the following permissions for specific functionality:

1. **Camera**: To capture new images for your documents
2. **Storage Access**: To access existing images from your device's gallery and save documents locally
3. **Internet**: Only used for loading external resources if needed (no data is sent)

All data remains on your device and is not shared with any third parties.

## Data Storage

All documents, images, and metadata created with DocKeep are stored locally on your device in the Pictures/Dockeep directory. No data is stored on external servers or shared with third parties.

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on your device's security measures. We recommend using device-level security features like PINs, passwords, or biometric authentication to protect your device.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
